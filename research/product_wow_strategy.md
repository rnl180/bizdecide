# Making BizDecide the product owners love and judges remember

Research synthesis, 10 Oct 2026. Built from nine cited research streams (the four products you named, US, Europe, China and Southeast Asia, India/Middle East/LatAm/Africa and the 2025–26 AI-CFO wave, Malaysia-specific hooks, agentic demo UX, brand naming) plus a verified supply/demand landscape sweep. Every claim below carries a link. Raw source reports with the full evidence are in [research/sources/](sources/).

Tags: **[V]** vendor page · **[T]** third-party press or review · **[O]** official/government · **[R]** peer-reviewed · **[I]** inference · **[VERIFY]** not confirmed on a primary page.

## 0. Eleven findings that change the pitch

1. **Two Malaysian vendors now use your exact words, and neither ships what you ship.** QNE Cloud was rebranded **N3 AI Accounting** in 2026 with a credit-metered chatbot marketed as "Quinny AI, your 24/7 AI-powered Virtual CFO" [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/quinny-ai-the-ai-behind-your-numbers/). mandrill.com.my is **Mandrill Tech Sdn Bhd**, an enterprise BI consultancy that on 3 Oct 2026 announced a "Lestar AI CFO Assistant" from RM1,500/month with two weeks of onboarding [V] (https://www.mandrill.com.my/blog/budget-2027-ai-malaysia-sme-cfo). Both stop at ratios, cards and worklists. Neither gives a decision with ringgit impact, evidence rows and a funding application pack. That gap is your demo.
2. **"cfo.a" is cfo.ai.** Runway Financial rebranded on 10 Sep 2026 and launched **Ari**, "an AI agent designed to act as a chief financial officer for startup founders" [T] (https://siliconangle.com/?p=837986). It costs $375–1,125/month, integrates NetSuite, Stripe and Salesforce, has zero customer quotes on its homepage and no Malaysian or SEA integrations [V] (https://cfo.ai/pricing). It is the best-designed product in the category and it is not for a kedai kopi.
3. **The category is crowded globally and empty for Malaysian micro-businesses.** Nume ("Hire Nume, your AI CFO" for 1–100 people, first assessment in ~3 minutes) [V] (https://www.nume.ai/), Finoya ("Free forever" for small businesses) [V] (https://finoya.ai/), Wyzr (UK, Product Hunt Apr 2026), Mastercard's "virtual CFO" for small businesses (Mar 2026) [T] (https://www.fortune.com/2026/03/10/small-businesses-cant-afford-finance-chief-mastercard-virtual-cfo-ai), Intuit's six QuickBooks agents, Xero's JAX. None does Malaysian funding matching, Bahasa, or a document pack.
4. **Every product people love does the same four things.** It shows diagnosis → attribution → action with a money number; it puts the evidence beside the claim; the AI stops and asks when unsure; it names its limits. Meituan's merchant AI is praised as "a business health check-up" [T] (https://www.chinanews.com.cn/cj/2026/06-26/10647834.shtml); Digits' most-repeated moment is the AI saying "I have this as taxes, but I actually think it could be legal" [T] (https://earmarkcpe.com/four-years-of-cleanup-in-four-hours-inside-the-ai-ledger-that-learns-from-your-work/).
5. **Your hero feature is already proven in the US restaurant market.** MarginEdge compares every new invoice to past ones "so increases show before they reach the P&L", ranks "Price Movers" by total impact, and emails the owner the moment a price is off [V] (https://www.marginedge.com/food-cost); an owner: "it will alert us immediately once the invoice has been entered". Nobody in Malaysia does this.
6. **Malaysia changed under your feet this year, and that is a gift.** The e-invoice exemption rose to RM3m on 1 Sep 2026, its third change in 15 months [O] (https://www.hasil.gov.my/e-invois/garis-masa-pelaksanaan-e-invois/). Budget 2027 was tabled on 9 Oct 2026: MSME tax 14% on the first RM150k from YA2027, RM2,000 minimum wage from June 2027 with MSMEs under RM50m sales exempt, RM6.6bn microfinance [T] (https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2617655). TEKUN went online on 7 Aug 2026 with a four-document checklist that includes three months of bank statements [T] (https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/business-loan/tekun-financing-applications-go-online-with-approvals-targeted-in-five-days.html). An agent that knows yesterday's budget is a live knowledge base, not a slide.
7. **A visible, explained mistake builds more trust than a perfect run.** In a 2025 two-study experiment, one visible advisory error caused a large trust drop, and a post-error explanation drove recovery above baseline [R] (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12561693/). First-person uncertainty wording ("I'm not sure, but…") reduced over-reliance in a pre-registered N=404 study [R] (https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00623). Plant one error for the Verifier to catch on stage.
8. **Judges will score the demo as much as the AI.** The AI Hackerdorm rubric is not published anywhere reachable [VERIFY]. Tencent's comparable Singapore 2026 challenge weights AI innovation 30%, user experience and demo 25%, business value 25%, technical 20%, and demands a "live-runnable demo, with a screen recording as backup" [T] (https://abu-dhabi.aitinkerers.org/hackathons/h_bKcMFTi7cB8). Tencent's own stated value for agents is "可信、可控" (trustworthy, controllable) [T] (https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260727A032W100).
9. **Use the Claude API for analysis and keep WorkBuddy as the front door.** It is allowed under the 10 Oct organiser rule, it removes the biggest live-demo risk (a third-party test scored WorkBuddy 2.31/4 and users report "偶有报错重试和卡顿", occasional errors and lag [T] (https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2654008710)), and the honest hybrid still earns sponsor points. Details in section 6.
10. **BizDecide is legally clean and brand-weak.** Zero trademark hits at MyIPO and WIPO; bizdecide.my, .com.my, .ai and .app are free; bizdecide.com is parked for sale. The strongest alternatives are **Tauke** (brand) with **Kak Kira** (persona), or **Tentukan**. "CFO" is not a restricted title in Malaysia; "financial adviser" and "financial planner" are. Section 7.
11. **Family-run SMEs are the sharper wedge, and nobody is building for them.** PwC's Malaysian Family Business Survey (49 decision makers, 2021) found only 24% report strong digital capabilities, only 45% say family members share similar priorities, and 57% say information is shared transparently [T] (https://www.pwc.com/my/en/publications/2021/family-business-survey.html). MIA's 2025 survey of 713 members found the absence of a clear business case is now the top technology-adoption barrier [T] (https://www.at-mia.my/2025/06/19/technology-in-motion-key-insights-from-survey-on-technology-adoption-by-the-accountancy-profession-in-malaysia-2025/). Accounting software digitises transactions without transferring the founder's decision logic. Every decision an owner accepts in BizDecide can become a dated, reasoned entry in a **decision log**, and the brief plus funding pack can double as a **succession pack**. Details in section 3.3 and [sources/11-malaysia-sme-accounting-study.md](sources/11-malaysia-sme-accounting-study.md).

## 1. The four products you named

### 1.1 insightsoftware (now Lineos) — why finance teams love it, and the one idea to steal

- **What it is.** A mid-market and enterprise "Office of the CFO" vendor, "34,000+ customers", split on 16 Sep 2026 into Lineos (finance), Certent and Simba [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/insightsoftware-launches-three-specialized-technology-brands/). Pricing is quote-only. Not a micro-business product.
- **Why people love it.** Excel-native ("If you can use Excel, you can use Spreadsheet Server" [V]), independence from IT, and above all drill-down: a G2 reviewer calls "drill down for more information is huge" [T] (https://www.g2.com/products/spreadsheet-server-by-insightsoftware/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons); the vendor's own line is "Click any number to see where it came from." [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/spreadsheet-server/). Time saved is the headline benefit: "Our team easily saves twelve hours a week" [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/jet/jet-reports/).
- **Their 2026 AI.** A reconciliation agent named Isaac marketed as a "virtual teammate" at "95% autonomy", delivering "finished work, not lists to review" with confidence scores and an audit trail [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/insightsoftware-introduces-a-virtual-teammate-with-justperform-account-reconciliation/). Reporting Intelligence (Jun 2026) alerts explain "what changed, why it matters, and what to do next" [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/lineos/solutions/reporting-intelligence/). They never say "AI CFO"; they say "companion" and "teammate".
- **What users complain about.** Slow and heavy, steep setup, data-trust failures ("never managed to pull through accurate data from our GL", Gartner 1/5, Jul 2025) [T] (https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/data-and-analytics/vendor/insightsoftware), contract pressure, opaque pricing.
- **Their own research is your trust argument.** 75% of 311 finance professionals say explainable AI would increase their confidence; 80% say trust concerns slow adoption [V] (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/new-insightsoftware-research-reveals-trust-not-technology-is-the-biggest-barrier-to-ai-adoption-in-finance/).
- **Steal:** every ringgit figure is a tappable chip that opens the CSV rows behind it. The decision card already has an evidence array; make it the headline, not the footnote.

### 1.2 cfo.ai (Ari), formerly Runway

- **What it is.** "Meet Ari, your personal CFO." Ari "builds the model from your actuals and shows you what changes" [V] (https://cfo.ai/). Startup $375/month billed annually, Growth $1,125, Enterprise with a "Forward Deployed Finance Partner" [V] (https://cfo.ai/pricing). Target: VC-backed startups (ARR, AEs, board decks).
- **Why people love it (as Runway).** Product Hunt tagline "The finance platform you don't hate." The most praised feature is the scenario sandbox: "easy to perturb the model and test counterfactuals"; a non-finance user said the demo video made him "feel comfortable (not a finance guy) with finance" [T] (https://www.producthunt.com/products/runway-financial). G2 4.8/5 from 38 reviews.
- **The wow moment.** The homepage demo asks "Can we afford to hire two AEs?", streams status lines ("Checking the latest actuals…", pulling Stripe, Salesforce, Rippling), and lands a plan card: ending cash, downside/base/upside, "Pays back in 11 months". A 7:04 AM proactive message reads "Overnight rebuild done, checks pass." with four bullets ending "Worth a look." [V] (https://cfo.ai/). Founder Siqi Chen: "A chatbot waits for you to ask it something, but that's not how a CFO works" [T] (https://siliconangle.com/?p=837986).
- **Design rules worth copying verbatim.** Answers must expose "what did the agent use?", separating connected-source values from assumptions; alerts must name the changed input, its impact and link to the model; too many alerts "create noise" [V] (https://cfo.ai/blog/finance-ai-agent). "A good simulation lets you learn by changing something." [V] (https://cfo.ai/blog/what-if-running-your-business-were-as-fun-as-playing-a-video-game).
- **Weaknesses.** Price and segment exclude micro-SMBs; no customer quotes yet; Runway cons include "if you don't know SQL, there's a learning curve" [T] (https://www.g2.com/products/runway-financial/reviews). No funding workflow, no PDF brief, no live owner questions.
- **Steal:** the proactive morning note, "from your CSV" vs "assumption (edit)" tags on every number, and one scenario slider per decision scoped to that decision only.

### 1.3 QNE Cloud → N3 AI Accounting (Quinny AI)

- **What it is.** QNE Software (since 2004, Seri Kembangan) force-migrated all users to N3 AI Accounting on 16 Jul 2026 [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/n3-ai-accounting-migration-from-qne-ai-cloud/). Claims "Trusted by 75,000+ Malaysian businesses" on every page but "over 50,000 business users" in its own llms.txt [VERIFY]. Prime RM62/month annual (promo RM24.80), RM80 monthly, up to Enterprise RM500, all excluding 8% SST, "No cancellation or refund" on the promo [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/pricing/).
- **Why Malaysians love it.** Native LHDN e-invoice inside the transaction screen with "near-instant 2-second validation directly with LHDN" [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/n3-lhdn-e-invoice-solution/); "easy to use and user friendly"; long relationships ("Using QNE System for almost 17 years") [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/); SST built in without add-on fees.
- **The AI.** Quinny AI is a credit-metered "Virtual CFO" with Report Analyzer, Report Designer and a Financial Advisor that "flags cash flow risks and generates collection worklists automatically", still in early access. Example prompts: "Why is profit down this month?", "What are my top three expenses?" [V] (https://qne.cloud/my/quinny-ai-the-ai-behind-your-numbers/). Credits scale with tier, so AI is an upsell lever. Chat memory is zero to three days on plans under RM180.
- **Structural gaps.** Bank feed is **UOB only** and takes 5–7 working days to enable [V] (https://n3-accounting-support-my.qne.cloud/support/solutions/articles/81000414072-how-to-link-uob-bank-to-qne-cloud-accounting-). No Bahasa Melayu or Chinese UI. No DuitNow or TnG reconciliation. No push alerts, digests or WhatsApp delivery documented. Not found on the MDEC Digitalisation Partner panel where AutoCount and SQL are [VERIFY]. Complaints: "customer service sometimes is slow and unresponsive", "the training on how to use the product feels too generic" [T] (https://softwarefinder.com/accounting-software/qne-accounting).
- **Positioning line:** "N3 is a 20-year-old ledger that bolted on a credit-metered chatbot. BizDecide starts from the owner's question and hands back three ringgit-quantified decisions, in Bahasa, from whatever exports they already have. No migration, no bank-feed waitlist."

### 1.4 Mandrill Tech (Lestar "AI CFO")

- **What it is.** Petaling Jaya BI/AI consultancy, founded 2015, "150+ professionals", clients AEON, PLUS, Bank Muamalat; hero line "Turn Your Business Data Into Decisions — Not Reports" with "Results in 6–12 weeks" [V] (https://www.mandrill.com.my/). Their AI CFO is "an intelligent software layer that sits between your scattered financial systems and your decision-makers" [V] (https://www.mandrill.com.my/blog/what-is-an-ai-cfo-and-why-malaysian-finance-teams-are-adopting-them-in-2026): nightly pulls from ERP, Xero and bank feeds, text-to-insight chat linking answers to GL lines, anomaly flags, 30/60/90-day cash forecasts.
- **Pricing.** Their own benchmark for SME-tier AI CFO software is RM36,000–72,000 a year plus RM15,000–30,000 implementation [V] (https://www.mandrill.com.my/blog/ai-cfo-vs-hiring-a-finance-analyst-cost-speed-and-accuracy-compared). The new assistant "starts from as low as RM1,500 a month", onboarding "as little as two weeks" [V]. That is 20× N3 Prime.
- **What they do well.** Evidence-linked answers (every insight ties to GL entries), Bahasa Malaysia queries, ringgit-quantified use cases ("27% price variance", "RM2.8M procurement saving") [V] (https://lestar.ai/use-cases), a "sensitive names masked before analysis" privacy promise, and the framing that a dashboard "waits for you to check it".
- **Weaknesses.** Built for RM20–200m companies with finance teams and ERPs. No self-serve trial, no screenshots, no reviews anywhere, no LHDN/SST coverage, no WhatsApp, no funding pack.
- **Positioning line:** "Mandrill sells an AI CFO to companies that already have a CFO. BizDecide gives one to the 1.1 million Malaysian micro-businesses whose CFO is the owner's WhatsApp and a shoebox of receipts."

### 3.2 Claims to drop or rephrase

- "Competitors require manual data entry." All three incumbents do OCR and bank reconciliation (research/competitors.md). N3 does 2-second LHDN validation.
- "GrabFood eats 30%." Vendors disagree (15–22% vs 25–30%). Say "15–30% depending on tier" and compute from the owner's own rate.
- "AI accountant" or "akauntan AI". The Accountants Act 1967 restricts accountant titles [VERIFY text]. "AI CFO" is safe (section 7).
- "98% WhatsApp open rate." It is a vendor estimate; the measured benchmark is about 68% [T].
- OZÉ as an "AI coach". Its coaching is human; the robo-advisor is a stated future goal [V] (https://solve.mit.edu/challenges/good-jobs-and-inclusive-entrepreneurship/solutions/29407).
- Gamification, streaks, badges. Tencent's gamified merchant app sits at 2.3/5 [T].
- Any "approved" wording in the funding ladder. Matched, eligible, document-ready only.
- "Market leader" claims about incumbents. No independent market-share survey exists; the defensible wording is "widely deployed, based on vendor-reported installed bases": Access UBS 500,000+ Malaysian SMEs, SQL 320,000+ companies across Southeast Asia, AutoCount 240,000+, Bukku 65,000, all vendor claims with different scopes (sources/11). QuickBooks Online Malaysia's official pricing page shows no native MyInvois or SST filing [VERIFY], which is a useful aside if a judge names it.

### 3.3 The family-business angle: decisions as a succession asset

The Malaysian SME accounting study in [sources/11](sources/11-malaysia-sme-accounting-study.md) makes a point none of the product scans did. In a family-run kedai or contractor, customer credit rules, supplier terms, pricing exceptions and cash priorities live in the founder's memory. Software preserves transactions but not decision logic, so a successor "receives reports without the context required to act". Formal workflows are resisted when they feel like policing and accepted when framed as continuity protection.

BizDecide already produces the raw material: each decision carries a title, action, reason, evidence and an accept/reject from the owner. Three small additions turn that into a wedge no incumbent occupies:

- **Decision log.** Every accepted or rejected decision is stored with date, reason, the owner's answer to the live question, and who approved it. Over months it becomes the written version of "how Abah runs the shop". The study's requirement list names exactly this: "record material pricing, credit, inventory write-off and related-party decisions with reason and approver".
- **Succession pack.** One export: the decision log, the supplier list with terms and the price history, recurring obligations (SST, zakat, EPF dates), adviser contacts, and the funding packs already prepared. The study calls for "export chart of accounts, process maps, key policies, recurring obligations, adviser contacts and access handover checklist".
- **Owner view vs accountant view.** The brief page is the owner view (cash, receivables, exceptions). A share-with-accountant link that exposes the verification log and source rows is the accountant view. The study: "cash, receivables and exceptions for owners; reconciliations and audit detail for finance users".

The demo line: "Every decision Aminah accepts becomes the handbook her daughter inherits." It costs little (the data is already in the Result schema) and it answers the judge who asks what happens after the first brief.

## 6. Engine choice: Claude API for analysis, WorkBuddy as the front door

**It is allowed.** Since 10 Oct 2026 Track 2 requires proof of WorkBuddy use plus a how/where/scenario explanation, not integration (organiser clarification, recorded in the team's notes).

**Why move the analysis.** A third-party 70-question test scored WorkBuddy 2.31/4 and a reviewer on Tencent's own product page reports "偶有报错重试和卡顿" (occasional errors, retries and lag) [T] (https://www.itiger.com/hans/news/2654008710; https://cloud.tencent.com/product/workbuddy). A live three-minute demo on a flaky orchestrator is the single largest risk in this plan. The Claude API gives a deterministic, replayable run with structured JSON that maps directly onto the existing Result schema, streaming for the live step feed, and parallel tool use for the six-lane visual.

**Why keep WorkBuddy visible.** Judges are Tencent-adjacent. The Hong Kong WorkBuddy-track rubric asks "Did the team use sponsored products?" [T] (https://luma.com/agentcreativity) and the Tencent Singapore rubric weights "Depth of AI Utilization" at 30% [T]. Tencent's rewarded primitives are project spaces with parallel agents, reusable experts, Skills on SkillHub (OpenClaw-compatible), MCP connectors and WeCom/WeChat remote triggering [V] (https://cloud.tencent.com/product/workbuddy). The one Tencent-native finance precedent is Chanjet's nine "digital employees" (finance assistant, procurement agent querying 1688 prices), among the first apps on WorkBuddy's open platform on 2 Sep 2026 [V] (https://www.chanjet.com/news/136372.html); WorkBuddy lists no small-business finance expert of its own. Publishing the price-watch and funding-match skills on SkillHub and showing a messaging-triggered run would make BizDecide that expert.

**Recommended split.**
- Analysis agents (Bookkeeper, Procurement, Classifier, Funding) and the Verifier: `claude-opus-5-5`. Thinking is always on; set `output_config.effort` to `high` for the Verifier. $4 input / $20 output per million tokens.
- Bulk row work (categorising ~1,300 purchase lines, exact-duplicate detection): `claude-haiku-5-5` at $0.10 / $0.50 per million. A full Kedai Kopi Aminah brief should cost well under RM5 even if everything runs on Opus [I].
- Result JSON through structured outputs (`output_config.format`), which validates against the existing Zod schema. Forced `tool_choice` returns a 400 on Opus 5.5, so do not port that pattern. Enable the server-side `fallbacks: "default"` beta.
- Streaming for the live lanes; `display: "summarized"` on thinking if you want a readable reasoning line in the Verifier lane.
- WorkBuddy keeps: the skill that triggers a run and receives the brief, Ask-your-CFO, scheduled delivery to messaging, and the SkillHub listing. The slide says "Claude agents do the analysis. WorkBuddy is where the owner's CFO lives." Never say "better than WorkBuddy" on stage.

**Rule guardrails for the Classifier and compliance checks** (from the review of the team's tax and compliance knowledge base in [sources/11](sources/11-malaysia-sme-accounting-study.md); these belong in the agent instructions and the market pack, whichever engine runs them):
- Never code "revenue under RM3m = exempt". Ask whether the entity has a non-individual shareholder, is a subsidiary or holding company, or has a related company at or above RM3m. If unknown, return "cannot determine", never "exempt". This is exactly the kind of question the live poll exists for.
- Report headroom as ringgit (threshold minus revenue), not as a ratio; a ratio is utilisation.
- Keep every compliance rule versioned with its source URL and effective date, and show that on the card (the study calls this an "evidence ledger").
- Keep Budget 2027 proposals (14%/16% MSME rate, RM2,000 wage floor) in a separate "proposed" bucket from in-force defaults until the Finance Act is verified. Show them as "tabled 9 Oct 2026, not yet law".
- Classify the service before applying an SST rate; a binary "F&B" flag is not enough to decide 6% versus 8% or whether rent is taxable.
- Route ambiguous deductibility and personal-versus-business items (the Harvey Norman purchase) to the owner question, never to an automatic disallowance.
- Use official contribution schedules for EPF, SOCSO and EIS where tables apply, not flat percentages.

Rejected with evidence: Kira alone (kira.ai is Litera's legal AI, kira.com.my is a political cooperative, 27 MyIPO marks, "kira-kira" means "approximately"); Untung (untung.ai shows "Launching Soon © 2026", all domains taken; UntungLah is the fallback); Jimat (also means amulet); Tiga (tiga.ai is a live product); Decidr (decidr.ai is live); Cerdik/Akal (cunning/trick senses).

**Persona rules (ANNA's pattern).** Three fixed tics: she says the ringgit number first; she names the supplier who raised prices; she closes with "Tentukan: ya atau tak?". Keep "CFO" in her on-screen title.

**Is "AI CFO" a restricted term?** No. The Financial Services Act 2013 restricts "financial adviser" (defined around insurance products; holding out as an authorised person carries up to 10 years or RM50m) [O] (https://www.bnm.gov.my/regulations/financial-advisers). The Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 restricts "investment advice" and "financial planning" (up to RM10m or 10 years) [O] (https://www.sc.com.my/regulation/licensing). "CFO" and "chief financial officer" appear in neither statute as a protected title. The Accountants Act 1967 restricts "accountant" titles [VERIFY text]. Practical rules: never write "financial adviser / penasihat kewangan", "financial planner / perancang kewangan", "licensed", "approved by BNM/SC" or "AI accountant"; keep recommendations to the owner's own operations (costs, prices, suppliers, cash) and government-scheme information; add the line "Bukan nasihat pelaburan atau insurans berlesen / Not licensed investment or insurance advice." Get a Malaysian lawyer before commercial launch.

### 9.2 Demand side: what SMEs want and cannot get from existing systems

**Software records the past; owners want next steps.** KRI and MDEC call MSME digitalisation "largely broad at the surface and shallow in depth" (2026) [T] (https://www.krinstitute.org/press-release/from-survival-to-scale-digitalisation-among-malaysias-micro-and-small-enterprises-remains-broad-but-shallow). The kedai owner persona "can't tell you if the business made money last year" [T] (https://www.timesofmalaya.com/articles/sme-bookkeeping-malaysia/). **Cash-flow blindness.** Only 9.8% of Malaysian SMEs call their cash flow healthy, 49% are under pressure, and 83.3% saw operating costs rise by more than 10% (SME Association of Malaysia, Jul 2026) [T] (https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/economy/2026/07/1481456/sme-credit-risk-rises-cost-pressures-intensify). This is the strongest demand evidence for Decision #1.

### 9.3 Lessons from those who failed or pivoted

- **Bench** (12,000 customers, shut Dec 2024): a "push to embrace AI" produced tools that "didn't work properly"; burned $135M and lost ~$30M on $42M revenue [T] (https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/05/bench-burned-through-135-million-before-shutting-down/). Customers' top post-mortem demand was data portability [T] (https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42523061). Rule: every ringgit links to source rows; CSV and PDF export is guaranteed.
- **ScaleFactor** ($100M+, shut 2020): "AI bookkeeping" done by accountants with books "filled with errors" [T] (https://research.g2.com/insights/scalefactor-raises-100-million-then-abruptly-shuts-down). Rule: state which steps are automated and which wait on the owner's answer.
- **Botkeeper** (~$90M, shut Feb 2026): claimed 98% accuracy, lost its largest clients [T] (https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/02/09/botkeeper-is-closing-its-doors/177677/). "In accounting, 95% correct is 100% wrong" [T] (https://www.countingworkspro.com/blog/botkeeper-shutdown-ai-accounting-automation). Rule: suggest-and-review, never silent auto-posting; publish an honest accuracy ramp.
- **Lummo/BukuKas and Khatabook**: free ledgers reached 6.3M and 50M users and could not monetise; "unreliable data" blocked lending [T] (https://themorningcontext.com/internet/khatabook-cant-lend-enough-to-justify-its-valuation). Rule: charge for a decision or funding outcome from day one; verify data before any lender sees it.
- **Fluidly**: cash-flow forecasting with 1,100 accountant partners survived only as a lender's data feed [T] (https://fintech-alliance.com/news-insights/article/oaknorth-set-to-close-fluidly-following-2021-acquisition). Rule: tie every forecast to a funding or pricing action.
- **Brex** dropped tens of thousands of small businesses that produced under 2% of revenue [T] (https://www.bankingdive.com/news/fintech-brex-reputation-hit-after-dropping-small-business-customers/636097/); Xero retired Xero Go for sole traders [V]. Pilot tops out near 60% gross margin with humans in the loop [T] (https://sacra.com/research/pilot-mechanical-bookkeeper); real-world auto-categorisation error runs ~33% on messy data [T] (https://aisbresources.org/articles/how-ai-is-changing-bookkeeping-economics). Rule: micro-SMEs must be served at software margins by asking the owner, not by a back office.

### 9.4 What this means for the pitch

1. Lead with "three decisions in ringgit", not with the "AI CFO" label; the label is crowded and Malaysian SMEs adopt AI on "tangible business value" (n=301) [R] (https://shdl.mmu.edu.my/13753/).
2. Make self-verification the headline feature: trust in AI decisions splits roughly a third for, a third against among Malaysian SMEs [T] (https://e27.co/xero-malaysian-smes-20251119/).
3. Two defensible slide lines: "only one in ten Malaysian SMEs says its cash flow is healthy" and "only a third of micro enterprises can get bank financing".
4. The supplier price-drift check is the feature incumbents have refused for four years (196 votes on Xero) and café owners get no supplier notice.
5. The funding pack must generate the exact artefacts lenders list: six months of transactions, a cash-flow projection, management accounts (CIMB, CTOS).
6. Encode scheme thresholds as deterministic rules (TEKUN's Bumiputera and paid-up conditions, DanaNITA's 51% women-owned and six months operating, GDPM's RM50,000 turnover) [V] (https://www.tekun.gov.my/?page_id=281; https://www.mara.gov.my/bm/menu_keusahawanan/kemudahan-sokongan/pembiayaan-perniagaan-2/dananita/).
7. Position against "spray and pray": "Applying to the wrong lender or product is a common mistake" [V] (https://blog.fundingsocieties.com.my/navigating-business-loans-in-malaysia/).
8. Do not claim "no AI finance chat exists in Malaysia": JAX is available to Malaysian Xero subscribers [V] (https://www.xero.com/my/jax/). Differentiate on CSV/POS input and quantified output.
9. Price near the micro anchor (Niagawan RM497/year) and justify any premium with the ringgit impact of each decision.
10. Avoid the folk statistics "82% fail from cash flow" and "43% lack visibility"; use the Fed Small Business Credit Survey 2026 (77% report rising costs, n=6,525) or BNM data instead [O] (https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2026/2026-report-on-employer-firms).
