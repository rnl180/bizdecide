## 2. Naming patterns in loved SME-fintech brands
- MY: Bukku "A Cloud Accounting Software for Malaysia, Finally." (buku = book); Biztory; senangPay "Still senang, now more powerful."; JomPAY/DuitNow (jom = let's; duit = money); Boost; Curlec; Funding Societies; CapBay.
- SG: Aspire, Sleek, Osome, Jaz ("Accounting Software Built for Agents"). ID: BukuWarung "Solusi Cuan Agen Pembayaran" (cuan = slang profit); majoo ("Lebih Maju"); Mekari.
- Global: ANNA "Absolutely No Nonsense Admin" (https://anna.money/about) ← clearest persona precedent; Pennylane; Agicap "Turn Cash Flow Chaos into Harmony"; Digits; Puzzle; Ramp "Time is money. Save both."; Kick "Self-driving accounting".
- Patterns: (1) one everyday word the customer already says; (2) Bahasa word + English noun in camel case; (3) a person, not a product; (4) imperatives/verbs; (5) money/outcome words (Untung, Cuan, Laba).
- Bahasa roots (DBP): kira = count ("menghitung (wang dll)"); untung = profit/luck; tauke = "tuan punya badan perniagaan (kedai dll)", Hokkien 頭家 thâu-ke "boss"; tentu/menentukan = "memutuskan… membuat keputusan".

## 5. Is "AI CFO"/"CFO" restricted in Malaysia?
- **No statute found restricts "CFO"/"chief financial officer".** Restricted: **"financial adviser"** (FSA 2013, BNM — defined around insurance products; s.28(1) holding out as authorised person: up to 10 yrs / RM50m; https://www.bnm.gov.my/regulations/financial-advisers) and **"financial planner"/"investment advice"/"financial planning"** (CMSA 2007, SC — s.58(1), Schedule 2 paras 5–6; up to RM10m / 10 yrs; https://www.sc.com.my/regulation/licensing). "chief financial"/"CFO" occur 0 times in FSA; only inside the "director" definition in CMSA. Accountants Act 1967 restricts "accountant"/"chartered accountant" titles [VERIFY — text not retrievable] → don't say "AI accountant / akauntan AI".
- Practical: "AI CFO" is a business-operations role (cost, pricing, supplier, cash decisions for the owner's own company), not securities/insurance advice. Keep it that way: no recommendations on securities, unit trusts, EPF/PRS, insurance/takaful. The scheme matcher is information/document assistance; avoid "we advise you which loan to take"; if commission-based loan arranging is added later, check Moneylenders Act 1951 + BNM intermediary rules [VERIFY]. Never use "financial adviser / penasihat kewangan", "financial planner / perancang kewangan", "licensed", "approved by BNM/SC". Add disclaimer: "Bukan nasihat pelaburan atau insurans berlesen" / "Not licensed investment or insurance advice." Low risk for a hackathon; get a Malaysian lawyer before commercial launch.
