## A. Malaysia-specific hooks
### A1. LHDN MyInvois
- LHDN timeline (updated 30/08/2026): Phase 4 "up to RM5m" from 1 Jan 2026; below RM3,000,000 exempt (https://www.hasil.gov.my/e-invois/garis-masa-pelaksanaan-e-invois/). History: Jun 2025 exempted < RM500k (https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2025/06/1226501/...); Dec 2025 Cabinet raised to RM1m; 20 Apr 2026 PM extended penalty-free relaxation to 31 Dec 2027; 1 Sep 2026 exemption RM3m under Guideline v4.8, with exceptions where a non-individual shareholder/holding/related company has ≥ RM3m revenue (https://www.3ecpa.com.my/resources/corporate-compliance-requirement/malaysia-e-invoice/). Consolidated monthly e-invoices due within 7 days of month-end; any single transaction > RM10,000 needs its own e-invoice from 1 Jan 2026; MyInvois e-POS for MSMEs early 2026; voluntary disclosure programme 7 Jul 2026–31 Dec 2027 (https://beancount.io/blog/2026/09/16/malaysia-e-invoicing-phase-4-rm3m-exemption-rm10000-rule-guide). 265,000 taxpayers had filed e-invoices by end-Aug 2026.
- **Demo move:** "e-Invoice status" card: "Declared sales RM X < RM3m → exempt today. But bank deposits show RM Y." Tie to the planted declared-vs-actual mismatch. Badge: "rule changed 3× since Jun 2025".
### A2. SST expansion (1 Jul 2025)
- New taxable services: rental/leasing, construction, education, financial services, healthcare at 6–8% (https://www.ey.com/en_my/insights/tax/malaysia-budget/sst-expansion-from-1-july-2025-...). Rental (Group K, 8%) threshold raised to RM1m. Sales tax 5% now covers ~4,800 previously exempt items incl. imported ingredients; rice, vegetables and milk stay 0% (https://www.storehub.com/my/blog/sst-expansion-malaysian-businesses-2025). SME groups want rental service tax cut to 4% (https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/09/01/a-people-and-business-centric-budget-wishlist).
- **Demo move:** when Procurement flags dairy +12%: "Milk is 0% sales tax. This increase is not SST pass-through; it is margin." For rent: "If your landlord is SST-registered, expect 8% on rent."
### A3. Schemes and Budget 2026/2027
- **TEKUN Niaga**: SPM RM1k–10k, SPK RM10,001–50k, SPS RM50,001–100k; target 5-day approval; 74.5% approved in 1–5 days; **online since 7 Aug 2026** at tekunonline.tekun.gov.my; docs: IC (both sides, plus spouse), SSM or council licence, premises photos, **3 months bank statements** (https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/business-loan/tekun-financing-applications-go-online-with-approvals-targeted-in-five-days.html; https://bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2591588).
- **MSME Digital Grant MADANI (BSN)**: 50% of invoice, max RM5,000; ≥60% Malaysian-owned; ≥6 months operating; ≥RM50k avg turnover; areas incl. ERP/accounting & tax, AI, e-invoicing; docs: IC, SSM, latest FS/management accounts **or 2 months bank statements**, TSP quotation (https://www.bsn.com.my/page/MSMEMadani).
- **BNM ADF**: up to RM3m, ≤4% p.a., 80% SJPP guarantee, via banks incl. Islamic banks (https://www.bnm.gov.my/funds4sme/adf). Agrofood RM5m ≤3.75%. SRF open until 30 Jun 2027; MEF for micro incl. gig workers (https://www.bnm.gov.my/funds4sme).
- **SJPP**: WCGS 70% ≤RM10m; WCGS-SU ≤RM500k; 2026: GGSM4 (https://sjpp.com.my/). **CGC**: BizJamin/-i, imSME portal (https://www.cgc.com.my/). **SME Bank**: Shariah Committee; 2019 checklist asks 3 yrs audited + management accounts (https://smebank.com.my/documents/d/guest/documentchecklist27022019). Typical bank/P2P: 6–12 months statements (https://blog.fundingsocieties.com.my/sme-loan-requirements/).
- **Budget 2026** (10 Oct 2025): RM50bn loans/guarantees; >RM2.5bn microloans via BSN/TEKUN; GoDigital MADANI RM150m for 50,000 SMEs.
- **Budget 2027** (9 Oct 2026): microfinance RM6.6bn (AIM RM3bn, TEKUN RM1.3bn, BSN RM1.7bn); SJPP+CGC guarantees up to RM32bn of RM57bn total; BNM SRF +RM5bn; MSME tax 14% on first RM150k, 16% on next RM450k from YA2027; min wage RM2,000 from June 2027, MSMEs < RM50m sales exempt; RM30m MDEC fund for 4,000 MSMEs to adopt AI/automation; halal-cert matching grant ≤RM5,000 (https://blog.fundingsocieties.com.my/budget-2027-malaysia/; https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2617655; https://fintechnews.my/61310/fintech-lending-malaysia/budget-2027-sme-financing-malaysia-rm57-billion/). ### A5. Zakat perniagaan
- PPZ-MAIWP: Modal Kerja = (current assets − current liabilities) × Muslim ownership % × 2.5%; Untung Rugi = (revenue − costs) × 2.5%, for small traders without accounts "such as restaurants and food stalls"; nisab 2026 = RM33,996 (85g gold); haul one year (https://www.zakat.com.my/info-zakat/jenis-jenis-zakat/zakat-perniagaan/). Individuals: rebate under s.6A(3) ITA 1967; companies: deduction capped at 2.5% of aggregate income (https://www.zakat.com.my/zakat-cukai/).
- **Demo move:** compute both: "Zakat perniagaan ≈ RM 2,1xx (Modal Kerja) vs RM 1,9xx (Untung Rugi); above nisab RM33,996; sole prop → rebate." One click, two numbers, official method named.

### A13. SME Corp definition and misclassification
- Micro = sales < RM300k OR < 5 FT employees; services small = RM300k–<RM3m OR 5–<30; medium RM3m–RM20m OR 30–75; manufacturing small RM300k–<RM15m OR 5–<75; medium RM15m–50m OR 75–200. Straddle → "the smaller size will be applicable". FT = ≥6 h/day, 20 days/month incl. foreign and contract, excluding working proprietors; only SSM/LLP-registered qualify (https://smecorp.gov.my/images/pdf/2024/Guideline_on_SME_Definition_March_2024_Final.pdf). Income Tax Act SME test differs (paid-up ≤ RM2.5m, gross income ≤ RM50m). KUSKOP redefining SMEs [VERIFY].
- **Demo move:** "Declared RM280k → micro. Bank deposits RM335k → small. This flips: BSN/TEKUN micro products, BNM MEF, DuitNow QR waiver." Live poll: "Which is right?" The single best use of the interrupt because it changes every downstream decision.
