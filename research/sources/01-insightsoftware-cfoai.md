# Why people love insightsoftware and cfo.ai — and what BizDecide can borrow

Research date: 10 Oct 2026. Labels: **[V]** = from vendor page, **[T]** = from third-party review/press, **[I]** = my inference. Anything I could not confirm is marked **[VERIFY]**.

**Method caveats.** G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner Peer Insights and Reddit block direct page fetches; G2/Gartner/TrustRadius quotes below come from search-engine snippets of those pages (URLs given), not full page reads. Reddit is not crawlable at all, so no Reddit evidence.

## 1. insightsoftware (Lineos / Jet Reports / Spreadsheet Server / Bizview / Power ON / Logi)

### What it is, who it's for, pricing
- **[V]** Portfolio vendor for the "Office of the CFO". Homepage hero: "Data in focus. Decisions you can trust."; claims "34,000+ customers" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/). On 16 Sep 2026 it split into three brands: **Lineos** (finance), **Certent** (equity), **Simba** (embedded analytics) (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/insightsoftware-launches-three-specialized-technology-brands/).
- **Segment:** mid-market to enterprise. Jet Reports targets Dynamics shops; Spreadsheet Server claims "140+ ERPs" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/spreadsheet-server/). Reporting Intelligence (Jun 2026) is the first product pitched at SMBs "without a data scientist, BI team, or IT project" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/lineos/blog/insightsoftware-launches-reporting-intelligence/). Not a micro-business product.
- **Pricing:** not published; quote-based (https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/reporting-tools/jet-reports/pricing). Third-party estimates $40–60/user/mo entry vs "~$1,200 per user, per year" conflict [VERIFY].

### What users praise most (third-party)
Jet Reports G2 4.6/5 (23) (https://www.g2.com/products/insightsoftware-jet-reports/reviews); Spreadsheet Server G2 4.4/5 (81); Gartner vendor average 4.5 (721 reviews) (https://www.gartner.com/reviews/vendor/insightsoftware).
1. **Excel-native, no retraining.** "seamless integration with Excel"; "very user friendly and easy to start for a newbie". Vendor: "If you can use Excel, you can use Spreadsheet Server."
2. **Independence from IT.** "reduces reliance on IT support".
3. **Live data, no exports.** "pulls live data without complex queries".
4. **Drill-down to the transaction.** "drill down for more information is huge". Vendor: "Click any number to see where it came from."
5. **Time saved is the headline.** Bizview TrustRadius: "reduce financial reporting time from days to minutes"; vendor customer: "Our team easily saves twelve hours a week" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/jet/jet-reports/).
6. **Responsive support** (Power ON Capterra 4.3/5).

### The "wow moment"
- Spreadsheet Server hero is a time promise: "Turn Hours of Manual Exports Into One-Click Report." + video "Watch Your First Report Built In Under 5 Minutes". Jet Reports hero: "Real-time Dynamics Reporting Directly in Excel", "75% faster month-end close", "90% less time building reports". Reporting Intelligence promises connection-to-insight in "60 minutes".
- Lineos hero: "Finance has never looked so good." "Instant demo" is a lead form, no self-serve demo (https://www.insightsoftware.com/lineos/demo/).
- [I] The wow is "your familiar Excel sheet, but the numbers refresh themselves and you can click into any of them".

### Complaints (openings)
1. Slow and heavy: "very CPU and Memory hungry"; "cannot use Excel while Jet Reports is running"; Power ON "limit screens to 3000 rows".
2. Steep learning curve/setup: "learning curve ... is steep" (https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1043745/jet-reports); SS "difficult setup process".
3. Data-trust failures: Gartner Jul 2025 1/5: SS "never managed to pull through accurate data from our GL"; Trustpilot 1/5 same.
4. Contract pressure / support decline: "insisted they sign up for another 3 years"; Logi "48 hour response time".
5. Opaque pricing.

### How they present insights
- Excel-first, scheduled distribution, Power BI dashboards, one-click drill-down.
- Reporting Intelligence "Intelligence Layer": threshold alerts, "autonomous insights" (anomaly detection, no config), "AI data assist"; smart alerts explain "what changed, why it matters, and what to do next"; branded "Your 24/7 AI companion" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/lineos/solutions/reporting-intelligence/).
- Reconciliation framed as "Get finished work, not lists to review." with confidence scores + audit trail; "Close 95%+ of reconciliations autonomously" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/resources/not-all-ai-reconciliation-is-created-equal/).
- No scenario sliders found.

### AI timeline 2024–2026
- Sep 2024 AI Doc Assist; Feb 2025 Jet Reports Online Doc/Data/Report Assist; 26 Feb 2025 "Lineos AI" (marketed as AI "line of sight", explicitly not "copilot"/"agent") (https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/insightsoftware-announces-ai-solution-lineos); Jul 2025 "from assisted AI to autonomous AI"/agentic workflows; 15 Jan 2026 "virtual teammate" reconciliation agent **Isaac**, "95% autonomy", "ready for approval" (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/insightsoftware-introduces-a-virtual-teammate-with-justperform-account-reconciliation/); 16 Jun 2026 Reporting Intelligence "always-on financial companion"; Sep 2026 Lineos brand, "agentic workflows", "auditable, eval-gated releases".
- Vocabulary: "assistant" → "agentic workflows", "virtual teammate", "AI companion". Never "AI CFO".
- Their own research: 75% of 311 finance pros say "explainable AI would increase their confidence"; 80% say governance/trust concerns slow AI (https://www.insightsoftware.com/blog/new-insightsoftware-research-reveals-trust-not-technology-is-the-biggest-barrier-to-ai-adoption-in-finance/). Earlier: 58% see AI as essential, only 39% confident using it.

## 2. "cfo.a" → cfo.ai (Ari), formerly Runway
- Runway Financial Inc. (founded 2020, Siqi Chen, $27.5M Series A 2023 — https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/17/runway-lands-27-5m-to-streamline-financial-planning-for-businesses) rebranded to cfo.ai on 10 Sep 2026 and launched **Ari**, "an AI agent … designed to act as a chief financial officer for startup founders and independent business owners" (https://siliconangle.com/?p=837986; https://ffnews.com/news/cfo-ai-launches-an-ai-cfo-built-for-founders-b684fc82).
- Other "AI CFO for SMB" products: **Finoya/"Noya"** ("Ask questions. See what is coming. Test decisions before you make them."; read-only Xero/Intuit; "Free forever" for small businesses; accountants $39/client after 5 free; https://finoya.ai/), **Wyzr** (UK AI CFO for small businesses, Product Hunt 28 Apr 2026, 76 upvotes; https://www.producthunt.com/products/wyzr/launches/wyzr-ai-cfo-for-small-businesses), **Agentic CFO** (Singapore; 0 G2 reviews), **EverCFO** Shopify app $79/mo, **CFOly** from $49, Mastercard "virtual CFO" for small businesses Mar 2026 (https://www.fortune.com/2026/03/10/small-businesses-cant-afford-finance-chief-mastercard-virtual-cfo-ai). [I] Category crowded but thin on reviews; nobody does Malaysia-specific decisions + funding packs.

### cfo.ai product, target, pricing
- Hero: "Meet Ari, your personal CFO." "Ari builds the model from your actuals and shows you what changes" (https://cfo.ai/). Integrations: NetSuite, QuickBooks, Rippling, ADP, Mercury, Brex, Stripe, Paddle, Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, Looker, Sheets, Excel. No Xero/SEA accounting.
- Target: VC-backed/growth-stage startups. [I] Upper-SMB, not micro.
- Pricing: Startup $375/mo annual ($500 monthly); Growth $1,125/mo; Enterprise custom with "Forward Deployed Finance Partner". 14-day trial; "Your first model, free" (https://cfo.ai/pricing).

### Praise (mostly Runway; Ari is 1 month old)
Runway G2 4.8/5 (38) (https://www.g2.com/products/runway-financial/reviews). Product Hunt 351 upvotes, tagline "The finance platform you don't hate." (https://www.producthunt.com/products/runway-financial).
1. Scenario sandbox: "sandbox where we can play around with different what-if scenarios"; "easy to perturb the model and test counterfactuals".
2. Makes non-finance people comfortable: "made me feel comfortable(not a finance guy) with finance".
3. Fast onboarding: "up and running within a matter of days, not months"; "very white glove onboardings".
4. Integrations "second to none". 5. Clean UI + support.
- No independent reviews of Ari found as of 10 Oct 2026 [VERIFY].

### The wow moment (https://cfo.ai/)
1. Hero demo: "Can we afford to hire two AEs?" → status lines "Checking the latest actuals", pulling revenue from Stripe, pipeline from Salesforce, payroll from Rippling → "2027 Growth Plan" card: ending ARR $17.90M, Q4 operating income $591K, ending cash $8.25M (−$46K vs plan), Downside/Base/Upside, "Pays back in 11 months", plus forecast table with a visible formula cell.
2. "It's not built for agents." → "We rebuilt spreadsheets for agents." (values addressed by dimension, e.g. `revenue[month=sep 26]`).
3. Proactive 7:04 AM message: "Overnight rebuild done, checks pass." with 4 bullets ending "Worth a look."
4. "A day with Ari": board-deck draft two weeks early; "What if we hire 30 salespeople now?" answered as an EBITDA bridge.
- Signup is Google/Microsoft/email code, no questionnaire. Docs: "Start with a business question and build a Model you can inspect and change." (https://docs.cfo.ai/).
- Siqi Chen: "A chatbot waits for you to ask it something" … "that's not how a CFO works" (https://siliconangle.com/?p=837986).

### Weaknesses / openings
1. Price and segment exclude micro-SMBs; no MY/SG integrations.
2. No social proof yet (zero customer quotes on homepage). Runway cons: "if you don't know SQL, there's a learning curve"; "can be unstable at times"; pre-Ari "lacks AI-generated suggestions or automation".
3. Founder-centric (board decks, NRR, AEs), not owner-centric. [I]
4. No funding/grant workflow, no PDF brief, no live-question verification loop. [I]

### How they present insights
- Chat answer + live model; scenarios separate from "Main"; Pages (shareable, password-able reports); **Canvas blocks** = custom mini-UIs per question (https://docs.cfo.ai/; https://cfo.ai/blog/what-if-running-your-business-were-as-fun-as-playing-a-video-game).
- Proactive Slack/MCP messages in base tier.
- Design rules: "A financial model should help you understand what happens when you change your mind." (https://cfo.ai/blog/why-runway); "A good simulation lets you learn by changing something."; agent answers must expose "what did the agent use?" (connected sources vs assumptions); alerts must name the changed input, plan impact, link to model; too many alerts "create noise" (https://cfo.ai/blog/finance-ai-agent).
- Marketing: "personal CFO", "AI CFO built for founders", "autonomous AI CFO", "works alongside founders 24/7". Not "copilot": the pitch is that it acts first.

## 3. Six ideas BizDecide should borrow
1. **"Click any number to see where it came from"** — every ringgit in a decision card is tappable → the supplier CSV rows (santan RM4.20→RM5.10/kg) with dates/invoice numbers; PDF gets an evidence appendix. (75% want explainable AI.)
2. **The 7:04 AM "checks pass" message + "what changed / why it matters / what to do next" alerts** — after each upload, a 4-line BM/EN morning note, WhatsApp-style: "Minggu ini: jualan RM18,400 (+6%). Stok kopi 9 hari sahaja. Hutang pembekal RM3,200 jatuh tempo Jumaat. Worth a look →". One alert per material change.
3. **"Learn by changing something" scenario sandbox** — 1–2 sliders per decision scoped to that decision only; "Buka Ahad?" shows weekly cash for 12 weeks; "Bayar pembekal lewat 14 hari" shows cash dip and discount lost; always show "Pays back in N months".
4. **"Finished work, not lists to review" + confidence + exceptions only** — verifier shows "412 of 418 rows matched (98.6%)" and turns only the 6 exceptions into live owner questions; the brief records what was auto-verified vs owner-confirmed as an audit trail for the funding pack.
5. **Live on the surface the owner already trusts** — for MY micro-SMBs: WhatsApp and Excel/Sheets. Export decisions + cash forecast as .xlsx with live formulas; "Ask your CFO" through WhatsApp with evidence links.
6. **Time-to-first-wow promise with visible agent steps** — "3 decisions in under 3 minutes from a CSV"; stepper (Reading 1,284 sales rows → Matching 9 suppliers → Checking 6 anomalies with you → Writing brief); demo starts from a real POS export with no sign-up form.
- Bonus: tag every number "from your CSV" vs "assumption (edit)"; separate "explained / proposed / changed". A bank officer or TEKUN reviewer asks this first.
