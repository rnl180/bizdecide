## 4. Patterns: why EU SMEs love these tools
1. Support humans with names dominate 5★ reviews; AI is loved when it shortens the path to a human.
2. Visible, reversible AI: "Copilot propose, vous validez"; "AI drafts the work, you review & approve"; "what was matched and why, with one-click undo". Opaque scoring gets punished (Capitalise).
3. One number that changes behaviour: money left after tax; 30-day balance; tax pot. Reviewers call it motivating.
4. Proactive beats reactive ("most founders don't even know what to ask").
5. Scenario toggles, not spreadsheets.
6. Regulation as the free hook ("included in all plans").
7. Warmth is a differentiator in a cold category, but only if the account doesn't freeze.
8. Chronic pain = integrations/bank feeds; data export lock-in angers.

## 5. Twelve ideas for BizDecide
1. "First assessment ready" in 60 seconds: three headline numbers (cash days left, gross margin, top overdue supplier) before the full brief (Nume).
2. Visible self-verification ("BizDecide Assure"): Verifier catches a double-counted credit note, strikes it through live, RM impact updates (JAX Assure).
3. "Copilot proposes, you decide" ladder: Accept / Ask-why / Not-now per decision; Accept writes to PDF + funding pack (Pennylane, Qonto).
4. Scenario toggles inside the live poll: "Hire 1 more worker at RM2,200?" → runway 11 → 7 weeks; "Delay the van 2 months?" → back to 12 (Agicap, Float).
5. "Duit selepas cukai" tile: cash vs cash after SST/income-tax reserve; decision "move RM1,350 to tax reserve now" (Accountable, Countingup).
6. Funding ladder with Swoop's honesty line: 140 schemes collapse to 3 with Matched → Eligible → Document-ready + missing-document checklist.
7. Explain the score, don't paywall it: "Fundability" gauge with the 3 blockers and the one action that flips each (Capitalise 1★ lesson).
8. WhatsApp digest "Minggu anda dalam ringgit"; owner types a rule in Malay ("Tell me when any customer is more than 14 days late") (Nume, JAX WhatsApp).
9. Suggested-question chips in Ask your CFO incl. coaching: "Coach me to ask Supplier X for 30-day terms" → script in BM (Xero JAX).
10. One-click "Share with accountant / bank officer": view-only link + comment box (Pennylane, sevDesk).
11. MyInvois readiness as a free hook: "You are RM1.1m from the RM3m threshold at current growth — crossing in ~14 months; supplier Aroma Sdn Bhd already issues e-invoices; import them."
12. A persona with a Malaysian wink ("Kak Ringgit"?), one signature sound/animation on accept, warm colour, zero jargon (ANNA). Keep light; reviewers forgive tone, not wrong numbers.
