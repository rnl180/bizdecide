## 3. Synthesis: why US users love these tools
1. Speed to first "oh" moment in minutes ("four years of cleanup in four hours", "In an hour… done", "hours, not weeks").
2. A dollar headline ("$30k in 30 minutes"; Price Movers "total impact"; "Savings Potential"; "$21,000 cash back").
3. Evidence beside the claim (PDF-beside-ledger hover; blue traceable numbers with formula; rationale + audit trail).
4. The AI admits doubt and asks ("I have this as taxes, but I actually think it could be legal"; Truewind garbled wire; Intuit agents "pause").
5. Proactive, not chatbot ("A chatbot waits for you…"; alerts "immediately once the invoice has been entered"; "what needs attention before month end").
6. Play, don't read (scenario sandbox).
7. "Why the numbers changed" narrative + stakeholder questions.
8. Nothing happens without approval, yet default is done-for-you.
9. Peer benchmark (Ramp; Nav "approval odds").
10. Complaints to design against: can't override AI mistakes; weekly fixing; heavy setup (7–10 days); fragile sync; pricey for micro; drift; lock-in.

## 4. Twelve ideas for BizDecide
1. Price Movers card with RM impact as Decision #1: "Syarikat Ah Seng raised biji kopi 12% across 3 invoices since Jun. Impact RM 1,860/yr at your volume. Show invoices ▸" + 6-month sparkline per SKU.
2. Evidence drawer on every number: hover "12%" → the 3 CSV rows, unit price before/after, formula.
3. Verifier agent speaks on stage: "Analyst says margin fell 4pts. Verifier: 14 Mar sales 3× normal — real or import error? Asking owner…"
4. One-tap poll questions: "RM 4,200 on 14 Mar — Raya catering / promo / mistake?" Tap → decisions re-rank live.
5. On-screen counter: "1,284 rows · 3 suppliers · analysed in 41s · 3 things need you".
6. Money headline above the fold: "RM 6,420/yr found in 3 minutes".
7. Scenario slider per decision: "Move 0–100% of beans to Supplier B" → margin and 90-day cash update live.
8. "Why the numbers changed" + "3 questions your banker will ask" in the PDF → feeds funding pack.
9. Funding-readiness score with fixes: "SME Bank / TEKUN / BSN micro readiness 72% — fix 2 things: 3 months statements, SST ID".
10. Accept / Snooze / Reject + audit log per decision.
11. WhatsApp digest / alert: demo ends with a ping "Supplier price up 12% — tap to see".
12. "Export everything" + "your data is yours" badge (Bench lesson).
Bonus: peer benchmark line ("kedai kopi in Klang Valley pay ~RM X/kg; you pay 14% more") with a clearly labelled synthetic demo cohort.

## 5. Demo-stage tactics
- Tell one owner's story with a number, not a feature list.
- Make a non-finance person feel safe: open on the owner's fear, close on relief.
- Anti-positioning line: "The finance platform you don't hate"; "Self-driving accounting"; "AI runs the numbers. You run the restaurant." → BizDecide: "Your CFO, for the price of a teh tarik" [INF].
- Let the AI be seen disagreeing with itself.
- Ask a live, specific question: "Boleh ambil seorang barista lagi RM 1,800/bulan?" answered with a cash curve.
- Validators in the room: a Malaysian accountant's 1-line quote on the slide.
- "Free-to-start" + "nothing happens without approval" were the two most-repeated launch promises.
