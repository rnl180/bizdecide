## Part 1 — Emerging-market micro-merchant apps
### India
- **Khatabook** (YC S18): digital bahi-khata, "Jama/Udhar" with free SMS/WhatsApp updates to the customer, auto backup, PDF statements, reminder dates; "Tally on steroids". App Store 4.7 (121,666 ratings); ~1M DAU, ~$3B transactions in 6 months post Dec-2018 relaunch [3P 2021] (https://ameya.substack.com/p/how-khatabook-became-the-premier). "50M+ merchants" [VERIFY]. Quote: "The easiest way to track my shop's finances"; complaint "The payment links tend to expire too quickly." (https://www.techjockey.com/reviews/khatabook). IVR bot-call reminders for paid users.
- **OkCredit**: udhar reminders auto over WhatsApp + SMS with a UPI pay link; payment from the reminder auto-entered; "customers don't need the app". Free basic; Premium ad-free + GST billing. "4.6 Loved by 1 crore+ shopkeepers on Google Play" (https://okcredit.in/payment-reminder-app).
- **Vyapar**: GST billing + inventory + WhatsApp bill/reminder sharing, **offline-first**, **9+ Indian languages**, lifetime-free mobile. 4.8 Play, "1.5 Cr+ businesses" [V]; Capterra 4.3 (181), 88% positive: "GST billing is much faster now", bills "sent directly through WhatsApp", "runs smoothly even on older budget smartphones" (https://vyaparapp.in/; https://www.capterra.com/p/180579/Vyapar/reviews/). Complaints: inconsistent support, "reminders sometimes get delayed".
- **myBillBook**: automatic payment reminders, recurring invoices; support over WhatsApp "resolved within minutes"; Capterra 4.4 (49).
- **TallyPrime 7.1 "TallyIra"**: "Docs by Ira" — send a document from desktop, mobile or WhatsApp, AI extracts and creates GST-compliant entries. "Connected GST" was the compliance hook.
- **Paytm for Business**: Soundbox voice-announces each payment in multiple languages; "monthly growth suggestions"; loans up to ₹10 lakh in ~5 min (https://business.paytm.com/).

## Part 3 — Synthesis
### What makes micro-merchants love a finance app
1. Udhar/fiado/hutang is the wedge; reminders are the hook; the customer never needs the app.
2. Vernacular, voice, WhatsApp-native (Vyapar 9+ languages, Soundbox voice, TallyIra via WhatsApp).
3. Offline-first on cheap phones.
4. Free core + monetise elsewhere (lending, payments, earn-commissions) — but Kippa and Finally show the monetisation leg can fail.
5. Records → score → money without an application (Foodics "You don't apply", OZÉ 3 months, Bumpa "skip the paperwork", CreditBook).
6. Compliance mandate as acquisition engine (Wafeq/Qoyod rode ZATCA; Tally rode GST; MyInvois is the MY equivalent).
7. Daily ritual + human nudge (OZÉ daily tip; Khatabook ~1M DAU).
8. Show the net, not the gross (Nu fee calculator).
### What the AI-CFO wave converges on
Approval gate + audit trail; ask-the-owner questions as first-class UI; pause where it matters; a feed/digest of what the agent did; delivery into chat tools; decisions with money + drill-down; external context (benchmarks, loan rates, negotiation coaching); human-in-loop sold as a tier; verification layer named and marketed (JAX Assure, Digits quality checks).
