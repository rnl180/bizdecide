CEO360: KPI range alerts, board views, multi-entity, "Fully configured and integrated by Mandrill Tech" (https://lestar.ai/products/ceo360). No self-serve trial, no screenshots, no customer names, no reviews; hypothetical cases. Zero LHDN/SST coverage. No WhatsApp, BM-first UI, PDF brief or funding pack.
