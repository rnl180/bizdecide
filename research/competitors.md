## What this means for your pitch (read this before writing the slide)

**Your brief's claim "competitors require manual data entry" is wrong in 2026.** All three now do OCR on receipts and some form of automatic bank reconciliation. If a judge has used Bukku, that line loses you credibility. Drop it.

What none of the three does, based on their own published feature lists:

1. **Tell the owner what to do.** They record and report. None flags "Supplier X has raised kopi powder 12% in 8 weeks without notice; here are two alternatives." This is your strongest differentiator.
2. **Match the business to financing.** Zero funding features. Given ~140 schemes and 50.6% unaware of DFIs (see market_stats.md), this is the second differentiator.
3. **Prepare the application evidence.** BNM's SME SRF asks for exactly the records an accounting tool holds: bank statements, invoices, supplier correspondence, sales records. Turning ledger data into an application pack is a gap no incumbent fills.

Positioning line: "Bukku tells you what happened. We tell you what to do next, and which of the 140 schemes will pay for it."

## Strongest objection a judge will raise, and your answer

*"Bukku or AutoCount can add an LLM feature in a quarter. What's your moat?"*

Answer with something concrete, not "we're AI-first":
- The funding knowledge base (eligibility rules as structured data, kept current) is tedious, non-core work for an accounting vendor and is the part that compounds.
- Integration instead of replacement: read from Bukku/AutoCount exports rather than asking the owner to switch ledgers. That turns incumbents into channels, not competitors.

## Gaps in this scan

- AutoCount and SQL "AI" add-ons have no public feature detail; a 10-minute trial signup would confirm whether either has insight/alert features [VERIFY].
- Other entrants not covered: Xero, QuickBooks, Financio, and POS vendors (e.g. FeedMe, StoreHub) that F&B owners already use. POS systems are arguably the closer competitor for a kedai kopi because they already hold sales data.
