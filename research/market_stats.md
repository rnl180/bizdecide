## 4. Suggested slide wording (all defensible)

- "1.1 million MSMEs, 96.9% of all businesses, 39.7% of GDP." (SME Corp/DOSM 2023 count; DOSM 2025 GDP share)
- "67% of SMEs say rising costs are their top pressure; 1 in 5 blame supplier price increases." (Airwallex 2026)
- "About 140 financing schemes exist, yet half of businesses don't know the DFIs that run them, and only 1 in 3 micro firms has any bank facility." (Ministry, Aug 2026; ACCCIM M-BECS H1 2026)

## 5. Threats to validity (have an answer ready if a judge asks)

1. Mixed years: the establishment count is 2023, GDP share is 2025. Say so if asked; DOSM has not published a newer count we could find.
2. ACCCIM respondents are chamber members, skewed to Chinese-owned and larger firms than a typical kedai kopi. The micro-firm figure (33.3%) is therefore probably an overestimate of access, which strengthens your argument.
3. Airwallex is a vendor-commissioned survey (n = 415). Treat as directional.
