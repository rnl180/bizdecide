# Owner interviews (item 7): script and capture sheet

You have to do these in person. Three 5-minute conversations near campus are enough for one "real voices" slide. Do them before dinner; owners are busy at meal times.

## Opening (say this, ~20 s)

"Hi, we're students at UPM building a tool for small shops for a hackathon. Can I ask you 4 quick questions about how you manage your accounts? No names, nothing recorded unless you say OK."

Get verbal consent. Do not record audio or take photos without it. Write down only first name or "Owner A/B/C". Do not write IC numbers, phone numbers or exact income, even if offered (PDPA 2010 applies to personal data you collect [VERIFY-MY]).

## Questions (in this order)

1. **"How do you track money in and out today?"**
   Probe: buku tulis? Excel? POS? Accounting app (which)? Who does it, how often?
2. **"Do you have an accountant? Roughly how much a year?"**
   Probe: only at tax time? Is it worth it?
3. **"Has a supplier ever raised prices on you? How did you notice?"**
   Probe: Did they tell you, or did you see it on the invoice later? How long after? This is the key question for the price-creep feature. Listen for "only noticed when profit dropped".
4. **"Have you heard of government financing like TEKUN, BSN or SME Bank? Have you applied? Why or why not?"**
   Probe: too many forms? Don't know which one? Rejected? Don't want debt (riba concern)?

Close: "Last one: if an app could warn you when a supplier quietly raises prices, would you use it? Would you pay RM30 a month?"

## How to use this honestly in the pitch

- Quote them as "a kopitiam owner in Serdang" etc. Never invent or polish quotes.
- Three interviews are anecdote, not evidence. Pair each quote with a statistic from market_stats.md.
- If an owner contradicts your thesis (e.g. "I check prices every week"), keep it. Showing you heard disconfirming evidence reads as rigour to judges.
- If you run out of time and get zero interviews, cut the slide. Don't substitute made-up "personas" presented as real people.
