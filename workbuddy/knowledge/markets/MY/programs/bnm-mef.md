# Micro Enterprises Facility (MEF) / Skim Pembiayaan Mikro

**Agency:** Bank Negara Malaysia via participating FIs
**Type:** Microfinance loan
**Official / source URL:** https://www.bnm.gov.my/microfinance
**Source type:** Official
**Checked:** 2026-10-10

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | RM1,000 – RM100,000 |
| Rate / cost | Varies by bank (BNM list as at 27 Jul 2026), e.g. BSN Mikro/i Madani 3.5%/4.0% flat; Bank Islam iTEKAD Maju 4% effective; Bank Rakyat MUsK MEF 6.5% flat |
| Tenure | Mostly up to 5–7 years |
| Ownership | Malaysian (product-specific) |
| Bumiputera only | N |
| Women only | N |
| Age | ~18–70 depending on product |
| Minimum operating period | 6 months to 3 years depending on product |
| Minimum turnover | not stated |
| Other eligibility | Microenterprises, social enterprises, professional service providers, self-employed incl. gig workers; no collateral (third-party guarantee may be required) |
| Sectors | All |
| Deadline | Ongoing |
| Status | Open |
| How to apply | Participating FIs (BNM list as at 27 Jul 2026): Agrobank, Alliance, AmBank, Bank Islam, Bank Muamalat, Bank Rakyat, BSN, CIMB Islamic, Maybank, Public Bank, RHB (+ UOB) |

## Required documents
- Identity card of the applicant (or of owners/partners/directors)
- Proof of business operations (business registration / licence / permit)
- Proof of income: usually latest 6 months bank statements, sales records, or latest Form B/BE with tax receipt
- Other documents set by each bank, e.g. Maybank: latest 2 years audited or management accounts; Bank Islam iTEKAD: completed application form and a utility bill

Source: BNM Skim Pembiayaan Mikro page, per-bank lists (as at 27 Jul 2026): https://www.bnm.gov.my/microfinance

## Notes
Range of effective rates is wide (AmBank 13.5–18% effective). Agent should rank by effective rate. BSN Mikro/i Madani has a Madani Wanita variant for women entrepreneurs. Documents differ by bank; the bnm.gov.my/microfinance page lists each bank's own list.
