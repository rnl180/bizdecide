# SME Stabilisation Relief Facility (SME SRF)

**Agency:** Bank Negara Malaysia via participating FIs; guarantee by CGC/SJPP
**Type:** Concessionary loan (BNM Fund for SMEs)
**Official / source URL:** https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/6025157/20261009-FAQ-SRF-EN.pdf
**Source type:** Official (BNM SME SRF FAQ, latest update 9 Oct 2026; also https://www.bnm.gov.my/funds4sme and brochure https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/6025157/SME-SRF-Brochure-EN-8.pdf)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | RM0 – RM750,000 |
| Rate / cost | Max 3.75% p.a. incl. 0.5% guarantee fee |
| Tenure | Up to 5 years |
| Ownership | ≥51% Malaysian |
| Bumiputera only | N |
| Women only | N |
| Age | n/a |
| Minimum operating period | not stated |
| Minimum turnover | not stated |
| Other eligibility | Viable but materially affected by trade/supply-chain disruption from the Middle East conflict (since Mar 2026); working capital only; no refinancing; up to 80% guarantee from CGC or SJPP, no collateral; RM750k cap across all banks; open to new and existing bank customers; RM10 billion facility |
| Sectors | All |
| Deadline | 15 May 2026 to 30 Jun 2027 or until fully utilised (BNM FAQ latest update 9 Oct 2026 and brochure; the 15 May 2026 FAQ said 31 Dec 2026) |
| Status | Open |
| How to apply | Any participating bank / Islamic bank / DFI; decision target 7 business days |

## Required documents
Each bank sets its own list (FAQ Q10). Generally:

- To show a viable track record: bank statements and transaction records
- Account conduct data (CCRIS)
- Audited financial statements or management accounts
- To show material impact: sales records, invoices, purchase orders
- Supplier contracts and supplier correspondence showing cost increases, delivery delays, cancellations or deferments
- Stock movement records, and debtor or creditor ageing reports

Source: BNM SME SRF FAQ, Q10 (latest update 9 Oct 2026): https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/6025157/20261009-FAQ-SRF-EN.pdf

## Notes
Deadline conflict resolved: BNM's FAQ (latest update 9 Oct 2026), brochure and funds page all say 30 Jun 2027; the FAQ now gives a RM10 billion facility (the 15 May 2026 FAQ said RM5 billion). Working capital only, so it cannot fund renovation or equipment capex. Strong demo hook: supplier price-rise invoices ARE the 'impact evidence' BNM lists.
