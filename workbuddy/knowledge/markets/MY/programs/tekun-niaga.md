# TEKUN Niaga (SPM / SPK / SPS)

**Agency:** TEKUN Nasional
**Type:** Microfinance loan
**Official / source URL:** https://www.tekun.gov.my/?page_id=281
**Source type:** Official (TEKUN Niaga 'Syarat-syarat' page, updated 28 Nov 2025)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | RM10,001 – RM100,000 |
| Rate / cost | 4% of financing value per year (flat, for the whole tenure) + savings of 5% of financing value per year |
| Tenure | Up to 10 years (SPM and SPK up to 5 yrs; SPS up to 10 yrs) |
| Ownership | Malaysian citizen, Bumiputera; companies 100% Bumiputera-owned |
| Bumiputera only | Y |
| Women only | N |
| Age | 18-65 at application; financing must end by 65 |
| Minimum operating period | not stated (application form covers both existing and new businesses) |
| Minimum turnover | not stated |
| Other eligibility | Bumiputera Malaysian citizen; not bankrupt; companies 100% Bumiputera- and Malaysian-owned; valid PBT licence/permit or SSM registration; fixed, mobile or online business location; owner directly involved full- or part-time; one financing per household; paid-up capital ≤ RM300k; Shariah-compliant activity; group financing takaful mandatory; SKSPS self-employed social security mandatory for year 1. First financing: SPK RM10,001–50,000 or SPS RM50,001–100,000; SPM RM1,000–10,000 is for repeat/overlapping financing only |
| Sectors | All Shariah-compliant |
| Deadline | Ongoing (no deadline stated) |
| Status | Open (ongoing scheme) |
| How to apply | TEKUN branch counter near the business, or TEKUN Online (tekunonline.tekun.gov.my); 03-9059 8888 |

## Required documents
- Copy of IC of applicant and spouse, if applicable (front and back)
- Licence/permit from the local authority (PBT) or SSM business registration
- Photos of the business showing it in operation
- Copy of the latest 3 months savings or current account bank statements showing the applicant's/company's name, bank name and account number
- Additional documents, if applicable

Source: TEKUN Niaga 'Syarat-syarat' page, 'Dokumen asas yang diperlukan' (updated 28 Nov 2025): https://www.tekun.gov.my/?page_id=281

## Notes
Old source URL (English conditions page) now returns 404. That page said age 18–60, ≥20% own cash for new businesses and other business financing ≤ RM100k; the current official page does not list those conditions. Rates are flat on original principal, so effective rate is higher (RinggitPlus, Jun 2026). One-time wakalah fee from RM50 to RM500 depending on amount.
