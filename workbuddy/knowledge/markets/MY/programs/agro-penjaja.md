**Type:** Membership initiative: micro financing + Cashline-i + Ar-Rahnu

**Source type:** Official (Agrobank product page + programme T&C and FAQ PDFs, May 2026; MRP 2.0 page https://www.agrobank.com.my/product/microenterprise-special-relief/)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10

## Required documents
- Membership (Agro Tani Saudagar-i account, individual): MyKad (or other listed ID) and a valid PBT business licence
- Membership (Agro Perdana Saudagar-i, non-individual, Peninsular): SSM registration certificate; IC copies of authorised signatories; partnership deed if any
- Micro financing (MRP 2.0): completed financing application form
- Copy of IC of applicant (and joint applicant, if any)
- Latest 12 months savings account statements or business sales records
- Copy of business registration or business licence (for fixed premises)
- Any proof of income the bank accepts
- Cashline-i (if applying): IC copy; plus 6 months sales receipts/invoices OR 6 months bank statements OR latest income tax form with LHDN receipt

Most F&B-specific scheme in the DB. Membership gives access to offers; each financing still needs its own approval. T&C section 2 names MRP 2.0 as the related micro-financing product. agrobank.com.my serves an incomplete TLS certificate chain, so some automated fetchers fail on it.
