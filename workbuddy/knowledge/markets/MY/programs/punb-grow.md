## Required documents
- Personal Data Protection (PDP) form (available on the PUNB online portal)
- Latest photocopy of applicant's and directors' IC
- Latest passport-size photo of applicant and directors
- Latest SSM search (printed within 2 months of submission)
- Photos of the existing/proposed business premises and products
- Valid tenancy agreement (if applicable)
- Management account for the current year and/or latest 2 years audited accounts
- Latest 6 months bank statements
- Copy of quotations according to project cost
- PUNB may request more documents for credit assessment
