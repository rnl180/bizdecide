## Required documents
- Copy of applicant's IC (salinan kad pengenalan pemohon)
- Copy of latest 6 months bank statements
- Copy of SSM registration / PBT licence / agency or professional-body registration
- Copy of home or business premises utility bill
- Other supporting documents requested by Hijrah Selangor

Source: Hijrah Selangor NIAGA PLUS page, 'Dokumen Permohonan' image (read 2026-10-10): https://hijrahselangor.com/pages/niaga-plus
