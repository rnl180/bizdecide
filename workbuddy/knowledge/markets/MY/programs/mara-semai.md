## Required documents
MARA does not publish a full checklist; applications go through the MyUsahawan portal (login required). Confirm the full list with MARA.

Items named in the official DanaNITA conditions:

- SSM business registration
- PBT licence / permit / permission letter (waived for financing ≤ RM50,000)
- Bank statements for 3 months, online statements accepted (listed under relaxed conditions)
- Quotation for renovation or machinery and equipment (waived for financing ≤ RM50,000)
- For RM50,001–RM150,000: guarantor details (fixed monthly income ≥ 3% of amount applied), land collateral, or a 10% Islamic fixed deposit
- SEMAi only: support letter from the head (Pengawal Pusat) of the centre where the applicant trained or studied
