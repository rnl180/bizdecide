# SME Cash Grant 2026

**Agency:** IRAS (MOF second support package, 29 Jul 2026)
**Type:** One-off cash grant (automatic)
**Official / source URL:** https://www.iras.gov.sg/schemes/disbursement-schemes/SME-Cash-Grant-2026
**Source type:** Official (+ MOF 29 Jul 2026 press conference)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10  

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | S$500 – S$2,500 |
| Rate / cost | S$500 per local qualifying employee, capped at S$2,500 per business. Sole proprietorships/partnerships with NO local qualifying employees: flat S$500 if the local owner's net trade income for YA 2025 is ≤ S$100,000. The grant is taxable |
| Tenure | n/a |
| Ownership | Registered in Singapore; only Singapore Citizen or PR employees count |
| Bumiputera only | N (not used in Singapore) |
| Women only | N |
| Age | n/a |
| Minimum operating period | Active and registered; still active at disbursement (Nov 2026) |
| Minimum turnover | not stated (annual revenue ≤ S$100 million for YA 2025, filed and assessed by IRAS as at 31 Aug 2026, OR ≤ 200 employees as at 30 Jun 2026) |
| Other eligibility | ACRA-registered companies, sole proprietorships and partnerships (incl. LLPs, LPs). Headcount = highest number of local qualifying employees (Singapore Citizens/PRs) in any one month of Apr–Jun 2026, counting only CPF contributions made on time (late ones excluded even if due to oversight). Shareholder-directors who are employees count; sole proprietors and partners are not employees. Businesses with local employees do not also get the flat S$500. Not eligible: ceased or inactive businesses, individuals employing workers under their NRIC |
| Sectors | All |
| Deadline | No application. Paid automatically in Nov 2026 |
| Status | Announced; automatic payout Nov 2026 (IRAS eligibility checker from mid-Oct 2026) |
| How to apply | Automatic. IRAS sends a letter to the registered address and a myTax Portal notice in Nov 2026 |

## Required documents
None: no application. To receive it, have an Income Tax/GST GIRO arrangement with IRAS or PayNow Corporate linked to the UEN (without suffix)

## Notes
Paid to help with higher energy prices and costs from the Middle East situation. Appeal details to be published closer to disbursement. Companies must declare it in Form C/C-S/C-S Lite; sole proprietors' returns include it automatically.

How the agent should use it: count only Singapore Citizen/PR employees with on-time CPF in the peak month of Apr–Jun 2026. Work Permit and S Pass holders do not count; the sole proprietor does not count. Multiply by S$500, cap at S$2,500. Nothing to apply for: tell the owner to make sure GIRO with IRAS or PayNow Corporate on the UEN is set up, and that the grant is taxable income.
