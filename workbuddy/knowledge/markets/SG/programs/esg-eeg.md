# Energy Efficiency Grant (EEG) – Base tier, Food Services

**Agency:** Enterprise Singapore
**Type:** Co-funding grant (reimbursement)
**Official / source URL:** https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/EEG
**Source type:** Official (+ FAQ https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/all-faqs/energy-efficiency-grant)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10  
**⚠ VERIFY the exact model on the GoBusiness equipment list (grants.gobusiness.gov.sg/support/energy-efficiency-grant/food-services returned 403; refrigerator rule seen only in a search snippet: regulated commercial storage min 4 ticks, other commercial refrigerators inverter)**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | not stated – S$30,000 |
| Rate / cost | Up to 70% of qualifying cost for SMEs, up to 30% for non-SMEs, until 31 Mar 2027. Cap S$30,000 per company for EEG Base equipment (1 Apr 2024 – 31 Mar 2027) |
| Tenure | n/a |
| Ownership | Minimum 30% local shareholding |
| Bumiputera only | N (not used in Singapore) |
| Women only | N |
| Age | n/a |
| Minimum operating period | Must have started operations at the location (current, valid tenancy agreement required for Food Services) |
| Minimum turnover | not stated (group annual sales turnover must be no more than S$500 million) |
| Other eligibility | Food Services: valid Singapore Food Agency (SFA) licence and classified under SSIC 56 or 68104. Equipment must be pre-approved and used in Singapore. Must not have commenced procurement, signed a contract or paid any deposit to the vendor before applying: the application is then treated as retrospective and not supported. Each equipment type or model needs a separate application; changing vendor after approval needs a new application. Food Services can use the Base tier only (Advanced tier is for manufacturing and construction) |
| Sectors | Food services, retail, manufacturing (incl. food manufacturing), construction, data centres, maritime |

| Status | Open (extended one year by Budget 2026) |

## Required documents
Quotations from the vendor (with a breakdown of fees); product brochures; tenancy agreement or contractual agreements for the deployment location(s), utility bills and telco bills; financial statements; corporate structure where shareholding is complex. At claim: invoice showing the approved model, proof of payment, delivery order, photos of the equipment installed and in use, serial number where applicable

## Notes
The EnterpriseSG page cites MOF's 7 Apr 2026 Ministerial Statement: the Base tier will expand to all sectors and run to 31 Mar 2028, details to follow (not yet in force). Each company's S$30,000 cap is shared across all its EEG Base applications.

How the agent should use it: confirm SFA licence + SSIC 56, local shareholding ≥ 30%, SME (70%) or not (30%). Grant = support % × qualifying cost, capped at S$30,000 per company. Work it out on the pre-GST quote: EDGE pages state GST is not supported, but the EEG pages read here do not say how GST is treated (VERIFY). Then search purchases/bank/payables for any payment or deposit to the same vendor dated before an application: if found, the application is retrospective and that purchase is not supported. Say so plainly and tell the owner not to pay deposits on anything else before applying.
