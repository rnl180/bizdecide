# Enterprise Financing Scheme – SME Working Capital Loan (EFS-WCL)

**Agency:** Enterprise Singapore via participating financial institutions
**Type:** Government risk-shared loan
**Official / source URL:** https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/enterprise-financing-scheme---sme-working-capital
**Source type:** Official (+ MOF 29 Jul 2026 https://www.mof.gov.sg/news-resources/newsroom/press-conference-on-second-support-package-in-response-to-the-continuing-middle-east-situation/)
**Checked:** 2026-10-10  
**⚠ VERIFY required documents with the lender (not published)**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | not stated – S$500,000 |
| Rate / cost | Interest set by the lender (subject to participating FIs' assessment of risk). Government risk share 50% (70% for young enterprises); 70% for ALL enterprises from 1 Sep 2026 to 31 Mar 2027. The borrower repays 100% |
| Tenure | Up to 5 years |
| Ownership | ≥30% local equity held directly or indirectly by Singapore Citizens and/or PRs (ultimate individual ownership) |
| Bumiputera only | N (not used in Singapore) |
| Women only | N |
| Age | n/a |
| Minimum operating period | not stated ('young enterprise' = formed within the past 5 years, at least 1 employee, more than 50% equity owned by individuals) |
| Minimum turnover | not stated (group annual sales turnover must not exceed S$500 million; SME = group revenue up to S$100m or up to 200 employees) |
| Other eligibility | Business entity registered and operating in Singapore; ACRA-registered sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs and companies may apply; approval subject to the lender's assessment; borrower group limit S$5 million for EFS-WCL |
| Sectors | All |
| Deadline | No closing date for the scheme. Enhanced 70% risk share: 1 Sep 2026 – 31 Mar 2027 |
| Status | Open |
| How to apply | Apply to any participating FI: CIMB, DBS, ETHOZ Capital, FS Capital, GB Helios, GXS Capital, HSBC, Hong Leong Finance, IFS Capital, Maybank, ORIX Leasing, OCBC, RHB, Sing Investments & Finance, Standard Chartered, UOB |

## Required documents
Not published by Enterprise Singapore; each participating FI sets its own list. Confirm with the lender

## Notes
The risk share changes how willing the bank is to lend, not what the borrower owes. Compare effective interest rates across lenders. The page's 'last updated' field is a placeholder.

How the agent should use it: eligible if registered and operating in Singapore with ≥ 30% local equity; the bank decides. Say clearly that the 70% risk share is temporary (to 31 Mar 2027) and that it does not lower what the owner repays. 'Young enterprise' (< 5 years) only matters after 31 Mar 2027.
