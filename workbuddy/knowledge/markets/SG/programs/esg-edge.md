**Agency:** Enterprise Singapore
**Type:** Co-funding grant (reimbursement)
**Official / source URL:** https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/edge-grant
**Source type:** Official (+ FAQ https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/all-faqs/edge-grant; activity page https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/edge-grant/automation-and-digitalisation/pre-scoped-equipment/automated-dishwasher)

## Required documents
Pre-scoped equipment: (1) vendor quotation with detailed scope, mandatory specifications, supporting licences/documents and product brochure where applicable; (2) projection of expected cost savings for one year after completion. Automation & digitalisation applications also need a valid, current tenancy agreement. At claim: clear photo of the installed equipment showing its serial number at the declared premises; delivery order where applicable; proof of full payment

## Notes
Old schemes: the EDG page says EDG, MRA and PSG ceased on 29 Sep 2026; the EDGE FAQ says no new applications after 30 Sep 2026. Projects already approved under them continue. Paid by reimbursement after full payment: PayNow Corporate about 14 working days, GIRO up to 8 weeks.

How the agent should use it: for a café, match equipment the owner wants (e.g. automated dishwasher) to an EDGE pre-scoped item, check the quote against the item's cost cap (S$5,000 for the dishwasher: grant = 50% of the quote excl. GST, max S$2,500), and check the bank/purchase data for any deposit already paid to that vendor. A deposit before applying makes the item unsupportable. EDG and PSG no longer take new applications: never recommend them for new projects.
