# Enterprise Financing Scheme – SME Fixed Assets (EFS-FA)

**Agency:** Enterprise Singapore via participating financial institutions
**Type:** Government risk-shared loan
**Official / source URL:** https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/enterprise-financing-scheme---sme-fixed-assets
**Source type:** Official
**Checked:** 2026-10-10  
**⚠ VERIFY required documents with the lender (not published)**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | not stated – S$50,000,000 |
| Rate / cost | Interest set by the lender. Government risk share 50% (70% may apply to young enterprises or enterprises in challenged markets). No 2026 enhancement listed for this facility. The borrower repays 100% |
| Tenure | Up to 15 years |
| Ownership | ≥30% local equity held directly or indirectly by Singapore Citizens and/or PRs (ultimate individual ownership) |
| Bumiputera only | N (not used in Singapore) |
| Women only | N |
| Age | n/a |
| Minimum operating period | not stated |
| Minimum turnover | not stated (group annual sales turnover must not exceed S$500 million) |
| Other eligibility | Business entity registered and operating in Singapore; ACRA-registered sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs and companies may apply; finances domestic or overseas fixed assets: equipment and machines for automation and upgrading (new or resale), purchase or construction of factories and business premises; approval subject to the lender's assessment. S$50 million is the maximum per borrower group across all EFS facilities |
| Sectors | All |
| Deadline | Ongoing (no closing date stated) |
| Status | Open |
| How to apply | Approach a participating FI (e.g. DBS, OCBC, UOB, HSBC, Maybank, ORIX Leasing); the page also points to the ESIMS portal (esgincentives.enterprisesg.gov.sg) |

## Required documents
Not published by Enterprise Singapore; set by the participating FI. Confirm with the lender

## Notes
The July 2026 70% enhancement covers EFS-WCL and EFS Project Loan only, not Fixed Assets. Some bank pages quote S$30 million per borrower group; Enterprise Singapore's page says S$50 million across all EFS facilities.

How the agent should use it: an option to finance equipment over a longer tenure than EFS-WCL. For small tickets (a few thousand dollars of kitchen equipment) say it is possible but usually heavier than paying the co-share from cash after grants.

> Amounts are in SGD. Save the official page from the URL above as `esg-efs-fa.pdf` in this folder before the demo if you want the original on disk.
