## Planted finding 3: Energy Efficiency Grant near-miss (deposit paid before applying)

- Goal: a new energy-efficient drinks chiller and an automated dishwasher, "using the Energy Efficiency Grant and EDGE". `profileExtras.grantApplicationsSubmitted` is empty.
- Chiller quote **CS-Q-2609-114** from CoolServe Refrigeration: S$6,800 + 9% GST S$612 = **S$7,412**.
- On **21 Sep 2026** the owner paid a **30% deposit of S$2,223.60** (purchases + payables: invoice CSR-DI-2609-114; bank: `PAYNOW TRF COOLSERVE REFRIGERATION DEPOSIT INV CSR-DI-2609-114`, ref PN0921S06).
- EEG rule (EnterpriseSG EEG page and FAQ): avoid signing a contract or paying before applying; a payment or deposit to the vendor before submission makes the application retrospective, and it **will not be supported**.
- What was lost: up to **70% × S$6,800 = S$4,760** of grant on the chiller (worked on the pre-GST price; the EEG pages do not say how GST is treated).
- A naive agent says "apply for EEG, get S$4,760". The correct agent says:
  1. The chiller as ordered is very likely **not fundable** under EEG because of the 21 Sep deposit.
  2. **Do not pay anything for the dishwasher** before the EDGE application is submitted. The same rule applies there, and S$2,300 is still available.
  3. Before re-applying for the chiller, check with the vendor whether the order can be cancelled and the deposit refunded, and ask EnterpriseSG through the BGP helpdesk or BizSG. Do not promise a grant.
- Malaysia comparison for the stage: the MY demo's funding near-miss is a turnover gap ("RM47k short of PUNB"); the SG one is a process rule that only shows up when the agent matches the bank statement against the grant rules.

### Programmes the agent should NOT recommend (and why)

- **PSG, EDG, MRA**: no new applications since 29/30 Sep 2026; replaced by EDGE (EnterpriseSG EDG page and EDGE FAQ).
- **NEA Hawkers' Productivity Grant**: needs a tenancy agreement with NEA or an NEA-appointed operator (hawker centre stalls), and NEA says the last application phase ended on 31 Mar 2026; the next phase is not yet announced.
- **Rental support for stallholders (S$1,200 per cooked food stall, Sep 2026 – Feb 2027)**: only for stalls in markets and hawker centres run by the Government or appointed operators. This café rents an HDB shop unit.
- **SkillsFuture Enterprise Credit**: the current credit is winding down (final claims due 30 Nov 2026 per the EDGE FAQ); the redesigned S$10,000 credit is announced for 2H 2026 but not confirmed open.

## Not anomalies (agent should NOT flag)

- Wah Sing Bakery invoices (WSB-1120 … WSB-1134) have no bank payment: **cash on billing** per suppliers/payables.
- Card/NETS settlements are 1.6% below gross card sales (merchant discount rate).

- The 30 Sep QR sales (S$435.07) settle on 1 Oct, after the statement ends.
